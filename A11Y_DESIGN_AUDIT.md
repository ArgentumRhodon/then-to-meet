# ThenToMeet: accessibility, design and product audit

Audited 2026-10-06 on branch `v2.0` (which contains `v2.0-users`), against WCAG 2.2 AA. Method: a code
read of every surface, then five passes in the built-in browser on the dev server (keyboard and focus,
screen-reader semantics, contrast and color, breakpoints/reflow/touch/motion, and roles and states),
using the demo event, the real poll `38870872-X4Rvz`, `/new`, and owner/admin/member/signed-out states
injected from the console. Contrast was computed (WCAG luminance, with semi-transparent layers and the
page glow composited), not eyeballed; the colorblind palettes were run through a Machado 2009
simulation. Nothing was committed, deployed, or written to Firestore.

## 1. Summary

**Verdict.** The app was already in good shape for mouse users with typical vision. The structure is
sound: one `h1` per view, labelled sections, native `<dialog>`s for every modal, `aria-pressed` on
toggles, text on the role chips, a lightness-ordered colorblind palette, `dvh` everywhere, and reduced
motion honored. The gaps were in a few places, and they were serious:

- the one task every visitor to a ThenToMeet event came to do (adding your times) couldn't be done
  with a screen reader;
- every popup menu was a half-built ARIA menu that dropped keyboard focus on the page body;
- several states were carried by color alone or sat under 3:1;
- the layout broke on short screens, at 200% text, and with long content.

Most of this is now fixed in the working tree. The remaining items need a product or visual
decision.

**Top 5 issues (all fixed unless noted)**

1. **Adding your times was unusable with a screen reader** (Blocker, F1). The availability picker
   declared `role="grid"` with no rows. Its cells were nameless, and moving or marking a cell
   announced nothing.
2. **Menus were half-implemented and lost focus** (Major, F2, F3). Settings, Share, Manage and the
   group menu used `role="menu"` but had no arrow keys and didn't move focus into the menu. Escape,
   or picking an item, dropped focus on `<body>`, about 60 Tab stops from where the user was.
3. **Focus was lost or stolen all over the workspace** (Major, F4–F8). The causes were:
   - buttons that disabled themselves after a press;
   - controls that removed themselves (Clear picked time, Show everyone, the group editor);
   - dialogs opened from a menu item;
   - Escape inside a dialog also clearing the page's picked time;
   - mouse hover over the heatmap taking focus and then dropping it.
4. **Color-only and low-contrast states** (Major, F12–F20). These were:
   - required vs optional names;
   - marked vs unmarked cells in the picker (2.0:1 in light);
   - the light-theme focus ring (2.2:1);
   - danger text in dark (2.7:1);
   - the heatmap cursor ring on the best cells (1.4–1.8:1);
   - links styled only by color.
5. **The default heat ramp gets darker at its top steps** (Major, F21, **fixed in round 2**, see section 0).
   The standard red→green palette's "everyone free" green is darker than the step before it, so the
   most important cells are the least distinct (OKLab L 75 / 76 / 74 at 5, 6, 7 of 7). This changes
   the palette the user chose, so it was left for them; they approved it.

**What was already good**

- The heatmap is one Tab stop with arrow-key navigation and a live readout. Escape backs out a step
  at a time, as documented.
- All four modal dialogs are native `showModal()`, so focus is trapped, Escape works, and they're
  labelled.
- Toggles and segmented controls expose `aria-pressed` inside labelled groups.
- Role chips say "Alex is required. Change to optional".
- The "New" and "Updated" markers have text, and so does the live indicator.
- The colorblind palettes do what they claim: lightness rises monotonically under each simulation.
- Every icon and avatar is `aria-hidden`.
- No `100vh`. Reduced motion is honored globally, including the Svelte transitions.
- Coarse-pointer targets are 40px. There's no page-level horizontal scroll at 320px at default text.
- Each role sees exactly the controls it should (section 3). The single-purpose rule holds: I found
  no admin control outside `SelectionManage` and `ManageMenu`.

## 4. Product notes (ranked)

1. **The timezone is invisible on the main screen.** Times appear with no zone anywhere in the
   workspace; the zone lives only in Settings. On a shared link this is the most likely way for
   someone to misread the answer. It's also shown four different ways: "New York · GMT-4" in
   Settings, "America/New York" in RespondDialog and `/new`, "(America/New York)" in copied text,
   and nowhere in the header. **Recommendation:** add "in New York time (GMT-4)" to the header's
   range line (it's already a summary line), and make it a link to the timezone view. Use one
   format everywhere.
2. **At laptop width the answer sits below the people list.** Between 1024 and 1279px, best times
   is placed under People in the sidebar, so on a 12-person poll "when should we meet" is below the
   fold, after roughly 50 controls. At ≥1280 it has its own column and reads well. **Recommendation:**
   at that width, put Best times above People in the sidebar, or fold People by default there as
   phones already do. This moves a panel, so it's left to you.
3. **The README describes features that don't exist.** It promises "an attendance slider" and result
   groups "one person short" and "fewer people". The panel has neither; it shows "Everyone" and
   "All required people", with near misses only as "Nothing fits" suggestions. Either the docs are
   stale or the features were dropped. **Recommendation:** update README "Purpose" and Key
   Features 2.
4. **The Undo pattern is fragile for deleting groups** (F10). An Undo that lives only in a toast
   can't be reached by most keyboard and screen-reader users in time. **Recommendation:** show the
   Undo inline in the GroupBar where the chip was, for the same 10 s.
5. **Terminology drifts.** Examples:
   - "event" vs "poll" ("No When2Meet event found" vs "Copy this poll into your account", "is in
     this poll anymore");
   - "Skipped" (chip) vs "Skip" (selection role) vs "Not counted" (tooltip);
   - "Delete" (groups, events) vs "Remove" (people, recents, picks) vs "Clear";
   - "responded" / "added their times" / "marked times";
   - "1h 30m" vs "30 min".

   **Recommendation:** use "event" for anything ThenToMeet shows and "poll" only for the When2Meet
   original. Use "Skipped" for the state and "Skip" only as a verb. Keep Delete for things that
   are gone for good and Remove for taking something out of a list (as now).

6. **"W2M" is jargon.** A header button labelled "W2M" (with an aria-label "W2M: open in
   When2Meet") means nothing to a new visitor. **Recommendation:** label it "When2Meet" with the
   external-link icon, and add "(opens in new tab)" to it and to Google Calendar.
7. **The "Manage" label in the selection card reads as admin-only.** For a signed-out visitor with
   a locked person selected, the section is titled "Manage" but holds only "Password…" (which
   anyone with the password may use, by design). That's fine, but "Manage" suggests they're an
   admin. **Recommendation:** none needed if owners are the main audience; otherwise retitle it
   "Entry" when only Password… shows.
8. **Single-purpose rule.** No violations found. Every person-level control is in `SelectionManage`,
   every event-level control is in `ManageMenu`, and RespondDialog is only name, password and times.
   One fix restored a user decision: the "Since your last visit" note used a **Sparkles** icon,
   which the user had rejected ("no gen-AI iconography"). It's now `History` (`PeoplePanel.svelte:5`).
9. **Long sidebar Tab sequence.** Three Tab stops per person: checkbox, eye and role chip. The eye
   is visually hidden until hover or focus but still a Tab stop. Now that there's a skip link this
   is acceptable. A roving tabindex in the people list would be the next step if lists of 50 or
   more are common.
